# CMS user guide

A practical guide to running the Butterfly Coaching Academy website from the admin. No technical knowledge is needed. Everything here also works on a phone.

Sign in at `/admin/`.

## The five things you will do most

| I want to... | Go to |
|---|---|
| Change text or an image on a page | **Pages**, open the page, open the section |
| Update a programme's dates, fees or hours | **Programmes**, open the programme |
| Answer a new enquiry | **Enquiries** |
| Publish an article | **Blog > New article** |
| Change the phone number, WhatsApp or email | **Settings > Contact details** |

## The dashboard

The first screen after signing in.

- **The welcome panel** says what is waiting for you, in one sentence, and opens the website in a new tab.
- **Six figures**: new enquiries, published pages, programmes and articles, approved testimonials and newsletter subscribers. Each is a link; a yellow edge means something needs action.
- **Quick actions** for the jobs you do most, including **Add a new course** and **Create a page**.
- **Latest enquiries** and **Recent activity** (the latest changes made in the admin, and who made them).
- **Needs your attention** lists only what really needs doing, such as testimonials to approve or pages with unpublished changes.

Every figure is read from the website's own records each time you open the dashboard.

## How saving and publishing work

There are two kinds of content, and they save differently.

**Pages** have a draft and a live version.

- **Save draft** stores your work. Visitors still see the last published version.
- **Preview** shows your draft exactly as it will look, at phone, tablet and desktop sizes.
- **Publish** makes the draft live.
- A page marked **Unpublished changes** has edits that are saved but not yet live.

**Everything else** (programmes, faculty, testimonials, FAQs, articles, settings) goes live the moment you press **Save**, as long as its status is Published (or Approved, for testimonials). Set the status to **Draft** to work on something privately.

## Pages

### Editing a page

1. **Pages**, then the page name.
2. The page is a list of **sections**. Select a section to open it and edit its text, buttons and images.
3. **Save draft**, then **Preview**.
4. Check the **Phone** view as well as **Desktop**. Most visitors will be on a phone.
5. **Publish**.

Every section adapts to the screen by itself. You do not design separate phone and desktop versions.

### Effects in headings and short texts

Headings, introductions and card texts can carry the hand-drawn effects of the November 2026 design. Type them around the words in the admin; visitors see the effect, and screen readers hear only the words.

| Type | You get |
|---|---|
| `==words==` | A yellow highlighter stroke |
| `++words++` | A lavender highlighter stroke |
| `__words__` | A thick hand-drawn yellow underline (lavender on dark sections) |
| `((words))` | A hand-drawn circle around the words |
| `[[words]]` | A tilted yellow tag |
| `**words**` | Bold |

A section heading with none of these gets the underline beneath its last word automatically, so every page shares the homepage's signature. The help text under each field says which effects it accepts.

On wide screens the effects draw in as they scroll into view; for visitors who ask their device for less motion, everything is simply shown.

### Buttons and links

A button has a label and a destination. The destination can be:

- a page on this site, such as `/contact-us/`
- a place on a page, such as `/about-us/#team`
- a full web address, such as `https://example.com`
- one of three shortcuts:
  - `booking`: your booking link, or the discovery-call form if you have not set one
  - `whatsapp`: a WhatsApp chat with your number
  - `phone`: a call to your number

Leave both boxes empty to hide the button. Using the shortcuts means that changing your number in Settings updates every button at once.

### Creating a page

**Pages > New page.** Give it a title and an address such as `/open-day/`, add sections, save and publish. To put it in the menu, add it under **Navigation**.

### Changing a page's address

If a live page's address changes, add a **Redirect** from the old address to the new one so existing links and search results keep working.

### Going back to an earlier version

Each page lists its **Published versions**. **Restore** copies an old version into your draft. Check it in Preview, then Publish.

### Search engines and sharing

Under **Search and sharing** on each page:

- **Search title**: what appears in Google and the browser tab. Aim for under 60 characters.
- **Search description**: one or two sentences, about 160 characters.
- **Sharing image**: the picture shown when the page is shared on WhatsApp or LinkedIn.

### Unpublish, archive, duplicate, delete

In the **Status** box. Core pages (Home, Contact and so on) cannot be deleted, only unpublished.

## Programmes

**Programmes** holds the facts about each programme. Change them once and they update on the programme's own page, on every programme card, and in the comparison table.

Important: **anything left empty is not shown.** If you have not confirmed a figure, leave the box empty rather than guessing.

- **Hours and format**: education, mentor coaching and experience hours; duration; format; languages.
- **Fees and dates**: the fee as you want it displayed, and cohort dates. Past cohorts stop showing automatically.
- **Accreditation**: the ICF badge appears only when the status is **Accredited (verified)**.
- **Images and brochure**: upload a PDF brochure and a download button appears on the programme page.
- **Curriculum, faculty and FAQs**: the step-by-step journey, who teaches, and questions specific to this programme.
- **Show on the homepage**: whether its card appears in "Explore programmes".

### Adding a new course

**Dashboard > Add a new course** (or **Programmes > New programme**).

1. **Programme name**, a **Short identifier** (for example `team-coaching`) and the **Page address** (for example `/team-coaching/`).
2. **Type**: *ICF credential pathway* puts it with the large cards and in the comparison table; *Course or service* and *Corporate programme* put it with the smaller cards beneath.
3. Fill in what you have confirmed: summary, description, hours, fees and dates, images. Anything left empty is simply not shown.
4. Set **Status** to **Published** and save.

The course now has its own page at its address, built from these details, and appears on the programmes page, in "Explore programmes" (tick **Show on the homepage**) and in the sitemap. To add it to the menu, open **Navigation**.

Want to design the page yourself instead? Create a page at the same address under **Pages**: a page built there always takes the place of the automatic one. That is how Mentoring, Coaching and Corporate work today; their Programme entry supplies the card.

## Testimonials

- New testimonials start as **Pending** and are hidden.
- Set **Approval** to **Approved** to show one.
- **Feature on the homepage** puts it in the homepage's "Student stories".
- **Short extract** is optional: one or two sentences copied exactly from the testimonial. The system will not accept an extract that changes the person's words.
- Use the **Internal note** to record who gave consent and when.

Only publish genuine testimonials, with the person's agreement. Never edit their wording.

## Blog

**Blog > New article.**

1. Title and **Address** (the article appears at `/your-address/`).
2. A short **Summary** for the blog index.
3. Write the article. The editor offers headings, bold, italic, lists, quotes and links.
4. Choose a **Featured image**.
5. Tick its **Categories** and **Tags**. Manage the lists with the **Categories** and **Tags** buttons at the top of the Blog screen.
6. Set **Status** to **Published** and save.

## FAQs

Each question belongs to a **Group** (`home`, `programmes`, `mentoring`, `corporate`). An FAQ section on a page shows one group. To start a new group, type a new group name on a question, then choose that group in an FAQ section.

## Media library

Every image and file on the site lives here.

- **Upload** JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF or PDF. Images are optimised automatically and location data is removed.
- Select an item to edit it:
  - **Alt text**: describe the picture in a sentence, for people who cannot see it. Items without it are flagged.
  - **Focal point**: click the most important part of the picture. Every crop, on every screen, keeps that part in view. The three previews show the result.
  - **Where it came from** and **Licence**: keep these accurate, especially for stock and AI-generated images.
  - **Replace file**: swap the picture everywhere it is used, in one step.
  - **Where is this used?** lists every page that uses it.
- **Delete** is refused for anything used on the live site. Replace it there first.

Filters at the top find items quickly, including **Missing alt text**.

Real people (founder, faculty, students) must always be shown in real photographs, with their consent.

## Enquiries

Every form submitted on the site arrives in **Enquiries**.

- **New** enquiries are shown in bold, and the menu shows how many are waiting.
- Open one to see the details, which page it came from and any campaign it came through.
- Reply by email, phone or WhatsApp from the links on the page.
- Update the **Status** as you go: New, In progress, Contacted, Qualified, Closed, Spam.
- Add **Internal notes** for your team.
- Filter by status or type, or search by name, email, organisation, message or campaign name.
- Enquiries that came through a tracked link show where from in the list (for example "via LinkedIn") and, on the enquiry itself, in one line under **Where this came from**.
- **Export CSV** (administrators) downloads the list for a spreadsheet.
- **Programme guide requests** come from the "Get the programme guide" forms on the homepage. They hold only an email address (shown in place of a name). Nothing is sent to the visitor automatically: reply with the guide by email, then mark the request Contacted.

If notification emails are switched on you also receive an email for each enquiry. If they are not, the enquiry is still saved here. The dashboard tells you which.

## Subscribers

People who signed up for the newsletter. **Export CSV** to import them into your email tool. Remove anyone who asks.

## Navigation

The header menu, its dropdowns and the footer link columns. Add, reorder, hide and remove items. Give a header item **Dropdown items** to turn it into a dropdown.

Keep the header to five or six short items so it fits on one line on a laptop.

## Redirects

Send an old address to a new one. Use **Permanent** when a page has moved for good, and **Temporary** when it will come back.

## Settings

| Tab | What it controls |
|---|---|
| Brand and logo | Site name, logos, favicon, default sharing image and description |
| Contact details | Phone, WhatsApp, email, address, optional map |
| Social links | Footer social icons (only those you fill in are shown) |
| Header and announcement | The yellow announcement bar and the header button |
| Need help panel | The "Need help?" button in the bottom corner: on or off, its heading, text and up to five options |
| Master classes | The classes people can enquire about on the enquiry page, and the text above its form |
| Footer | The short description and copyright name |
| Newsletter | Footer signup on or off, and its wording |
| Booking and analytics | Booking link; Google Analytics or Tag Manager; the consent banner |
| Colours and typography | The five brand colours and the typefaces |

**Announcement bar:** the yellow strip above the menu on every page. The quickest way in is **Dashboard > Edit the announcement bar**, which also tells you whether the bar is showing. Tick **Show the announcement bar**, write the text (for example "Next ACC cohort enrolling now · Limited seats") and set the **Announcement link** (for example "Reserve yours" going to `/level-1-acc-coach-program/`). The preview under the form shows the bar exactly as visitors will see it, or explains why it is hidden.

To show a date, write `{date}` where it belongs, for example "Next ACC cohort starts {date} · Limited seats", and pick the **Cohort start date**. The bar then stays hidden while there is no date and hides itself after the date has passed, so visitors never see a placeholder.

**Need help panel:** each option is a label, a link and a short description. Links can be a page address or the shortcuts `booking`, `whatsapp` and `phone`.

**Colours:** the page background is `#FCFCFC`, the grey-white of the BCA logo artwork, shared by the website and this admin. The **Readability check** shows whether text will still be easy to read with your chosen colours. Avoid saving a combination marked "Too low".

**Analytics:** nothing is tracked until you add an ID. With the consent banner on (recommended), visitors are asked first and nothing loads unless they accept.

## Links for posts and master classes

Every master class in **Settings > Master classes** has its own enquiry page, with the class already ticked in the form:

```
/enquire/?class=leaders-communication
```

Add tracking details to the end of that link when you share it, so each enquiry records where it came from. For a post on Anup's LinkedIn profile about leadership communication:

```
https://butterfly-coaching-website.vercel.app/enquire/?class=leaders-communication&utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=anup-communication-practice&utm_content=organic-anup
```

| Part | What it records | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `class` | Which master class is ticked. Use the class's **Short name for links** | `leaders-communication` |
| `utm_source` | The site the visitor came from | `linkedin` |
| `utm_medium` | The kind of link: `social` for an ordinary post, `paid` for an advert, `email` for a newsletter | `social` |
| `utm_campaign` | A name for this post, so its enquiries can be found later | `anup-communication-practice` |
| `utm_content` | Who posted it, or which version of the link | `organic-anup`, or `repost-company` when the Butterfly page reposts it |

Good habits:

- Use lower case and hyphens, no spaces.
- Give every post its own `utm_campaign`. To see every enquiry from one post, search **Enquiries** for that name.
- Where LinkedIn or WhatsApp shows a preview of the link, it shows the class name, its one-line description and its sharing image.
- Changing a class's **Short name for links** stops links you have already shared from ticking it. Add a new class instead.
- A link to a class that is hidden or does not exist still opens the enquiry page, with nothing ticked.

Campaign details are saved with an enquiry sent from the page the link opened. If the visitor has accepted analytics, they are also remembered while they look at other pages first. The Privacy Policy describes this under **Campaign information**.

## Your account

Click your name in the admin menu, or open `/admin/account/`. Change **Your name** (the dashboard greets you by it) or your password.

## Tips

- Write for a phone first: short headings, short paragraphs.
- Keep button labels to two or three words.
- Use the same label for the same action everywhere ("Book a discovery call").
- Do not publish a figure, credential or claim you cannot evidence.
- The sticky bar at the bottom always shows **Save**. If it says "Unsaved changes", you have not saved yet.
